County services

Where to get the services residents use most.

Business permits and licensing

Apply for or renew a Single Business Permit, and obtain liquor licences.

Where: Chief Licensing Officer, City Hall Annex, Mezzanine Floor

Healthcare services

Clinics, inoculation, ambulance, food hygiene and medical certificates.

Where: City Hall, Ground Floor Room 45 (inoculation); 3rd Floor Room 345

Agriculture and food security

Urban agriculture, animal husbandry, fish farming and food systems coordination.

Where: Green Nairobi sector, City Hall, 2nd Floor

Revenue and rates

Pay land rates and rent, and settle parking and clamping fees.

Where: Chief Accountant Rates, City Hall Ground Floor

Planning and building approvals

Building plans and inspection, billboards and adverts, survey fees.

Where: Development Control, City Hall Annex, 1st Floor

Birth and death certificates

Obtain birth and death certificates through the public health office.

Where: City Hall, 3rd Floor, Room 322

Markets and housing

Market stalls and county housing allocation and rent.

Where: City Hall Annex, 11th Floor

Libraries, sports and halls

McMillan Library, City Stadium and hire of Charter Hall and garden spaces.

Where: McMillan Library, Upper Hill; City Hall, Room 175
