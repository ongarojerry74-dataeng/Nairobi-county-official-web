About Nairobi City County

Background, direction and leadership.

County background

Nairobi City County is the creation of the Constitution of Kenya 2010 and successor of the defunct City Council of Nairobi. It operates under the Cities and Urban Areas Act, the Devolved Governments Act and other laws.

As Kenya's capital, the county provides a wide range of services to residents, including those previously offered by the City Council and functions transferred from the national government.

Vision

To make Nairobi the city of order and dignity; Hope and Opportunities for all.

Mission

To provide affordable, accessible and sustainable quality services, enhancing community participation and creating a secure climate for political, social and economic development through the commitment of a motivated and dedicated team.

Core values

Accountability, transparency, excellence, accessibility, integrity, responsiveness, equity and team work.
